Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Edge Forge handles information when you use the Edge Forge Android application.

Information We Collect

Edge Forge does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, or phone number.

The app may process basic app usage and device information necessary for app functionality, diagnostics, security, and advertising services.

Advertising

Edge Forge uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, including advertising identifiers, app usage data, device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data, in order to provide, limit, measure, and improve advertising.

Depending on your region and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be shown a consent message before personalized advertising is enabled.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent Choices

Where required by law, Edge Forge uses a consent management solution to request and manage your advertising consent choices. You may be able to change or withdraw your consent through the consent options shown in the app or through your device privacy settings.

Third-Party Services

Edge Forge may use third-party services that have their own privacy practices. The main third-party advertising service used by the app is Google AdMob.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Retention

Edge Forge does not directly maintain user account data. Data collected by third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled according to those providers' privacy policies and retention practices.

Children's Privacy

Edge Forge is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the issue.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: edgeforgeapp@gmail.com.
